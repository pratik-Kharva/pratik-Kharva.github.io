02Our story

A tradition passed down
through generations

Sukhadia began in Khambhat — the old port town on the Gulf of Cambay in Anand district — where our family built its name making Indian sweets, snacks and namkeen.

In 1996 the family moved the business to Vadodara, and it has been run here ever since. Today it is looked after by Ashok Sukhadia and Mehul Sukhadia, the latest of six generations to stand behind the same counter.

“Each sweet & snack lovingly crafted to bring back memories and make new ones.”

03The mark

Why there is a parrot
on everything we make

Our great-grandfather Jamnadas was given a parrot by a saint who was a friend of the family. The bird stayed with the Sukhadias for more than thirty years and became well known across the neighbourhood in Khambhat.

When it died, the family kept its likeness and made it the mark of the business. It has stood for family tradition, quality ingredients and pride ever since — recognised in India and the United States alike.
